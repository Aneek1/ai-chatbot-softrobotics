## Sampling and cleaning

- OpenLID: up to 100,000 lines per label and 1,000 per other language, shards read in seeded order, stopping when every label reached its cap or after 16 shards.
- Every label reached its cap.
- Cleaning uses the app's normalization (NFKC, invisible characters removed, whitespace collapsed), drops lines under 2 characters (74 dropped) and exact duplicates (72,270 dropped).
- Contamination: 7,543 lines removed because they match a FLORES-200 dev or devtest sentence (409,836 checked) or a Dakshina test sentence or word (24,899 checked).
- Synthetic rows: OpenCC conversions (up to 20,000 per Chinese label), crops of 1-3 words from 10% of natural lines, and code-mixed rows equal to 2% of natural lines.
- `val_select` holds up to 2,000 validation rows per label; every model is chosen on it.

## Limitations

- OpenCC conversions do not read like natively written Simplified or Traditional Chinese.
- Crops of 1-3 words are cut from sentences, not collected from real queries.
- Code-mixed rows are two whole sentences joined and labelled with the longer part; real code-mixing happens inside sentences.
- The contamination check matches whole lines after normalization. A FLORES-200 sentence embedded inside a longer OpenLID line is not caught.
- Where a split is marked "no" under source-disjoint, it was carved from the same sources as training, so its scores are likely higher than on text from new sources.
- Romanized Hindi and Urdu come from one source (Dakshina: Wikipedia sentences romanized by annotators), so they are split by hash and are not source-disjoint.
